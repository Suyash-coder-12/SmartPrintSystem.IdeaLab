How Smart Print Works

Welcome to Smart Print, a completely cloud-based document printing solution designed for speed and privacy. Instead of waiting in lines or sharing documents via insecure methods like WhatsApp or email, you can now upload your documents directly to our secure servers.

1. Upload or Scan

Select a PDF, Image, or Word document from your device, or use our built-in scanner to capture physical pages instantly.

2. Customize Settings

Choose your preferred color mode (Color / B&W), number of copies, and view the exact pricing before printing.

3. Print & Collect

Pay securely online or choose cash. Your document is instantly sent to the shop's printer queue for immediate processing.
